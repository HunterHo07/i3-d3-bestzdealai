- **92%** of consumers read reviews before making purchase decisions

## 🏢 Competitive Landscape Analysis

### Direct Competitors
**None** - No dominant reverse-deal, buyer-initiated platform exists widely

### Indirect Competitors

#### Traditional Marketplaces (Seller-First)
- **Shopee**: 343M+ users, strong in Southeast Asia
  - *Weakness*: Seller-centric, buyers must search
- **Lazada**: 130M+ users, Alibaba-backed
  - *Weakness*: Complex navigation, no buyer-initiated deals
- **Tokopedia**: 100M+ users, Indonesia-focused
  - *Weakness*: Traditional marketplace model
- **Facebook Marketplace**: 1B+ users globally
  - *Weakness*: No AI ranking, basic search functionality
- **Craigslist**: 50B+ page views annually
  - *Weakness*: Outdated UI, no smart matching

#### Service-Based Platforms
- **Fiverr**: $3.42B market cap, service-focused
  - *Weakness*: Services only, no goods marketplace
- **Upwork**: $3.1B market cap, freelancer platform
  - *Weakness*: Complex bidding, high fees
- **TaskRabbit**: Local services only
  - *Weakness*: Limited to task-based services

#### Price Comparison Sites
- **PriceGrabber**: Traditional price comparison
  - *Weakness*: Static listings, no negotiation
- **Shopping.com**: Product comparison only
  - *Weakness*: No seller interaction, outdated model
- **Google Shopping**: Search-based comparison
  - *Weakness*: No reverse marketplace features
